About Eric Brock

> M.Arch candidate at ASU focused on computational architecture_
View architecture portfolio ↗

Eric Brock is an M.Arch candidate at The Design School at Arizona State University. Through studio, technical coursework, research, and early writing, he is building the foundation for a future in computational architecture.

His path to architecture began at Catholic University of America, where he studied classical architecture and technical drawing before transferring to ASU. Prior to architecture school, he worked in undergraduate admissions at Johns Hopkins and Howard. That experience informs his interest in how technical knowledge is taught, accessed, and applied.
